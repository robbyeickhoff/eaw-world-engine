## 3. Composed world layout

### Arrival and opening reveal — south approach

Use the southern ridge/valley approach to conceal Central until the player emerges from a mountain transit terminal onto a high reveal bridge. The primary view faces north toward the stone crown of peaks.

The intended frame layers are:

1. **Foreground:** terminal exit, bridge rails, banners, and the first EAW symbol
2. **Center:** World Engine Central and the three-color beacon
3. **Skyline:** The Crown suspended beneath the dominant mountain peaks
4. **Edges:** hints of Titan's Reach, Cloudbreak Isles, and the Wild Beyond

The final arrival coordinate and facing must be selected by a walking-height visibility survey, not aerial screenshots.

### World Engine Central — middle valley

Central occupies the broadest connected valley shelves. It should be a layered vertical city, not a flat plaza.

Planned structure:

- Reactor/crater at the lowest practical central shelf
- Three-color Eickhoff beacon as the ground-level visual anchor
- Public transit concourse one layer above the reactor
- Three main terraces aligned to Crown, Compass, and Comet routes
- Bridges spanning natural dips rather than filling them
- Maintenance passages connecting naturally to the caves
- The Crown centered on the north-facing arrival composition

### Titan's Reach — northern stone crown

Assign the highest exposed-stone peak chain to Titan's Reach. Its fortress and fallen-titan silhouette should reinforce the natural skyline without replacing it.

Primary access: mountain railway or cliff elevator from Central.  
Secondary access: Erik's advanced climbing/mastery route.

### The Wild Beyond — forested eastern and southeastern slopes

Preserve the densest forested slopes for exploration, fossils, ruins, river paths, and Luka's expedition. Routes should weave through terrain rather than clear a broad roadway.

Primary access: expedition rail/vehicle route from Central.  
Secondary access: hidden footpaths and cave shortcuts.

### Sunken Kingdom — lake gateway

Treat the natural lake as the visible entrance to the Sunken Kingdom, not necessarily its complete footprint. A submerged gate, bathysphere station, or flooded tunnel can lead to a larger deep-water site if the lake survey proves too small.

Primary access: water transit from Central.  
Accessibility route: enclosed dry walkway or glass elevator for simple navigation.

### Emberfall Frontier — western cave-and-cliff system

Use exposed stone, deep openings, and natural ravines for an industrial frontier. The district should descend into the mountain, keeping large foundries and drill works visually separated from the green valley.

Primary access: heavy rail through a surveyed tunnel.  
Secondary access: maintenance route tied to the Null Sector mystery.

### Cloudbreak Isles — upper valley airspace

Place floating islands above the outer valley edge rather than directly over Central. This preserves the Crown's skyline dominance and creates aerial travel across the mountain amphitheater.

Primary access: lift tower and airship dock.  
Secondary access: Heidi's high-speed Comet route.

### Null Sector — concealed beneath Central

Reserve a protected underground volume beneath or beside Central. Do not excavate or route ordinary transit through it. Its entrance remains unmarked until the public districts and finale design are mature.

## 4. Transportation skeleton

Build no district landmark until these routes have surveyed corridors:

1. **Arrival Line:** concealed southern terminal → reveal bridge → Central
2. **Central Ring:** simple public loop serving the three main terraces
3. **Titan Line:** cliff elevator/mountain rail northward
4. **Wild Line:** accessible expedition route through the forest
5. **Tide Line:** Central → lake gateway → future deep-water connection
6. **Ember Line:** industrial rail into the western cave system
7. **Sky Line:** lift tower → Cloudbreak Isles
8. **Comet Circuit:** later cross-world racing route that intersects districts without replacing their main access

Main routes must be understandable without flight, parkour, or advanced controls.
