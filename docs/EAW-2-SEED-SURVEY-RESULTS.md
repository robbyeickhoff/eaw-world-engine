Candidate D is the recommended foundation for **EAW 2.0 — The World Engine**. It offers the best combination of immediate visual impact, usable development space, natural district separation, vertical transportation opportunities, and long-term expansion.

Candidate C has extraordinary biome contrast but less practical central space. Candidate A is the safest broad-building option but has a less distinctive identity.

### Recommended conceptual use

- **World Engine Central:** Broad valley shelves, using selective terracing rather than wholesale flattening
- **The Crown:** Suspended against the mountain skyline where it dominates the arrival reveal
- **Titan's Reach:** Highest exposed-stone ridge and peak chain
- **The Wild Beyond:** Forested valley wall and expedition corridor
- **Sunken Kingdom:** Lake as its public gateway, with themed transit to deeper water if required
- **Cloudbreak Isles:** Open air between or above the surrounding ridges
- **Emberfall Frontier:** Cave-and-cliff system with exposed stone and vertical industrial routes
- **Null Sector:** Deep cave system concealed behind service access

## Important compromise

Candidate D is not naturally flat. The design must embrace layered terraces, bridges, elevators, tunnels, and ridge routes. Early construction must not erase the valley into a flat platform. This constraint strengthens the World Engine concept when engineered intentionally.
